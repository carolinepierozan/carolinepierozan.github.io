My (main) responsibilities 👩‍💻

  • Management and leadership of Research Operations
  • Creation and facilitation of workshops for discovery, ideation, data analysis, persona, user journey, team building, sprint retrospectives, 1 on 1 and more
  • Creation, management, and documentation of tools, guides, frameworks, and templates using Notion and Miro as the repository
  • Collaboration with stakeholders including product managers, marketing, designers, and development team members, to define research questions and plans
  • Advocating research culture within the company with Product Managers and Design Teams, driving the organization to understand the customers' needs, challenges, and problems
  • Mentoring and assisting coworkers across the organization in their independent research efforts

And it was like this:

The challenges 🏃‍♀️

Some of my key goals being a UX Researcher and Research Operations Manager is to understand and explore problems, needs, and users’ expectations, as well as understand all of the discovered problems and prioritize solutions, and discover gaps in unmet needs that can generate opportunities (innovation). But the main goal (and challenge) for me is to assertively support, pass on all of the information gathered, and teach how I did it, so people will be equipped with the best tools to solve their own problems. Together we do it better, but freedom and autonomy are essential.

At Platform Builders I had many responsibilities, but at any company, the challenges of a ResearchOps person are the same (in general), it'll variate only on the intensity: some challenges will be more challenging or painful in one place than the other.

My biggest challenges

(to the fellow researchers out there: you’ll probably relate to this)

  • Keep track of all ongoing research;
  • Make other colleagues from other areas/BUs aware of my existence and understand that they can count on me to carry out quality research;
  • Getting people to document the research process correctly and in the right place;
  • Getting people to use the research repository.

How to deal and solve those challenges

Some challenges are easier to resolve than others, but it's all a process and sometimes things take time to settle down. Remember we are dealing with human beings.

Trust the process. ✨
